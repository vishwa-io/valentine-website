# Valentine's Day Letter 

A small cute Valentine’s website I made.

It starts with an envelope, opens into a letter, and asks a fun "Yes or No" question.
The "No" button runs away and "Yes" shows a sweet message with a cute cat gif.

Just a simple little surprise page made for fun ⋆˙⟡

## What it has
- Envelope opening screen
- Letter popup
- Yes / No buttons
- Runaway No button
- Cute gifs
- Heart click effects
- Works on phone too

## How to open
Just open this in your browser.
